# Bundled key sound assets

The four short OGG sound effects in `app/src/main/res/raw/` are from Kenney's Interface Sounds pack.

- `key_sound_crisp.ogg`: kenney-interface-click-001.ogg
- `key_sound_soft.ogg`: kenney-interface-select-001.ogg
- `key_sound_mechanical.ogg`: kenney-interface-select-002.ogg
- `key_sound_bubble.ogg`: kenney-interface-tick-001.ogg

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
